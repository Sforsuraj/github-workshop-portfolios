Civil engineering is the branch of engineering that deals with designing, building, and maintaining infrastructure used by society. Civil engineers work on things such as roads, bridges, buildings, dams, airports, railways, water-supply systems, drainage systems, and tunnels. In simple words: Civil engineering is about creating and maintaining the structures and infrastructure that make cities and communities function safely.
